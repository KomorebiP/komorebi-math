# Block 5 release

Version `0.5.0a1` implements the cyclic analytic and finite quantum realization layer.

## Analytic domain

The analytic namespace now provides executable forms of the Paper IV constructions.

### Cyclic projections

Coefficient vectors and callable analytic functions can be split into residue classes modulo a positive order. Analysis, reconstruction, and cyclic product convolution are implemented.

### Transfer and signed sections

The transfer sections solve the finite cyclic differential system generated by a shift with positive wrap. The signed sections solve the corresponding system with negative wrap. Finite companion matrices, derivative identities, addition formulas, and reconstruction certificates are available.

### Reciprocal families

The package computes reciprocal coefficients, logarithmic coefficients, transfer tangent coefficients, parameter convolution certificates, congruential inverse families, Appell polynomials, and theta polynomials. Exact rational arithmetic is used whenever the supplied parameters allow it.

### Zero diagnostics

Positive real zero representatives are computed from the asymptotic seeds by an exponentially scaled Newton method. The returned certificate checks ordering and numerical residuals. It does not claim that the finite list exhausts every zero. Finite Hadamard products, zero count asymptotics, reciprocal coefficient diagnostics, and truncated reduced zero zeta sums are provided.

## Quantum domain

The quantum namespace implements the finite dimensional layer of Paper VII.

### Seeds and symmetry

Quantum seed systems, Schmidt rank, local orbit dimension, finite group projectors, cyclic symmetry packets, charge certificates, and tensor charge refinement are implemented.

### Circuits and transport

Finite unitary circuits can be expanded over packet paths. The package reports coherent interference and block unitarity. Fixed packet leakage bounds, moving packet generators, direct rotations, and midpoint Kato transport are available with residual certificates.

### Channels and error correction

Kraus channels support application, composition, tensor products, Choi matrices, covariance checks, and packet blocks. Lindblad generators provide trace preservation and coherence sector checks. Quantum codes provide Knill Laflamme compression, syndrome construction, recovery channels, and finite recovery verification.

### Effective and Euclidean dynamics

Hermitian Feshbach reduction provides self energy, projected resolvent checks, upper half plane sign diagnostics, and the exact hidden memory kernel. Euclidean transfer operators provide finite reflection positivity. Periodic lattice Laplacians, heat traces, spectral dimension diagnostics, low mode dispersion bounds, constraint closure, and bounded Hamiltonian towers are implemented.

## Boundaries

The following are not claimed by this block:

* a certified global zero enumeration beyond the requested numerical representatives
* a proof of the complete zero asymptotic theorem by computation
* arbitrary infinite dimensional quantum channels or unbounded generators
* continuum quantum field theory
* anomaly free continuum constraints
* Lorentzian reconstruction
* an Einstein infrared limit
* a physical spin two sector

## Verification

Run the installed suites with:

```powershell
komorebi verify analytic
komorebi verify quantum
komorebi verify block5
komorebi verify all
```

The source distribution also contains `verification/block5_deep_audit.py`, which runs deterministic randomized checks across the analytic and quantum APIs.
