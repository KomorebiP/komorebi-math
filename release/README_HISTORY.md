# Historical release evidence

The inherited `BLOCK16_*` through `BLOCK21_*` files record their original builds and tests. They are not fresh attestations for Block 36 repair R1. They have intentionally not been rewritten. `release/predecessors/` contains historical distributions, not installation candidates for this delivery.

`RELEASE_ID.json` at the repository root identifies R1. Current artifacts are the single build-tagged wheel and single sdist in `release/dist/`. Current delivery evidence is supplied in the enclosing bundle's `repair/` directory. The stored Block 36 manual also describes its original snapshot; its hashes and counts are historical where they concern distributions or old release evidence.
